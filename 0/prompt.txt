Build under src/:
1. state/facts.ts: a discriminated union of fact types: run_started{runId,issueId}, workspace_created{baseCommit,branch,path}, baseline_recorded{exitCode,passed,failed,failingTests[]}, mandate_created, context_built, patch_proposed{attempt,diffHash}, gate_result{attempt,passed,failedRules[]}, tests_result{attempt,passed,failingTests[]}, validator_result{attempt,allCriteriaPass}, attempt_failed{attempt,failureClass:"blocked_policy"|"tests_failed"|"rejected_validator"|"error_transient"|"no_evidence"|"stuck_duplicate"}, budget_checked{exhausted}, needs_human{reason}, shipped{}. Every fact gets seq and ISO ts. appendFact writes one JSON line to runs/<runId>/facts.jsonl and never rewrites.
2. state/derive.ts: a pure function deriveStatus(facts) returning one of: working, blocked_policy, tests_failing, rejected_validator, budget_exhausted, needs_human, ready_to_ship, shipped.
